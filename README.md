# Multi-Agent Decision Support Platform

> An AI-powered platform where multiple intelligent agents collaborate to analyze complex problems, evaluate alternatives, and generate transparent, evidence-backed recommendations.

---

## Overview

The Multi-Agent Decision Support Platform explores a collaborative approach to artificial intelligence by replacing a single AI assistant with a team of specialized agents.

Each agent is responsible for a specific aspect of the reasoning process—researching information, assessing risks, simulating possible outcomes, evaluating ethical implications, and validating conclusions. Together, they produce recommendations that are more reliable, explainable, and comprehensive than those generated by a single model.

The platform is designed for scenarios where decisions involve multiple variables, uncertainty, and trade-offs, making it suitable for domains such as business strategy, healthcare, public policy, disaster management, and enterprise operations.

---

## Motivation

Modern AI systems excel at answering questions but often struggle with complex decision-making that requires structured reasoning, multiple perspectives, and transparent explanations.

This project investigates how a collaborative network of AI agents can:

- Break down complex problems
- Gather and verify information
- Analyze risks and uncertainties
- Compare alternative strategies
- Explain the reasoning behind every recommendation

The goal is to create AI systems that assist human decision-makers rather than replace them.

## Example Workflow

1. User submits a decision problem.
2. The Orchestrator assigns tasks to specialized agents.
3. Each agent performs its own analysis.
4. Agents exchange findings and challenge assumptions.
5. Evidence is consolidated.
6. Recommendations are ranked.
7. The platform presents the final recommendation with supporting evidence, confidence score, and reasoning.
